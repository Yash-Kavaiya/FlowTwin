# What the full warehouse twin does

Everything below is in the app at `http://localhost:8000`. "Real" means measured or observed; "simulated" means generated by the app and labelled so on screen.

| Feature | Where | Real or simulated |
|---|---|---|
| 3D facility: racking with stock, receiving and dispatch docks, trailers, conveyor with moving parcels, packing stations, charging bay, control room, branded walls and sign | Main view. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset | Scenery, not observed |
| Three use cases with their own floor and structures | Use case tabs | Scenery and story |
| Camera perception: detections mapped to aisle zones, temporal confirmation, stale or covered view goes UNKNOWN | Camera panel, zone labels | Real (needs a camera and a model) |
| Rerouting, hold at a wait point when no route exists, verified clearance before resuming | Main view, Order and decision | Decisions real; vehicle simulated |
| Operator pause and resume, reset | Order and decision | Simulated vehicle |
| Operations: five extra vehicles (a tugger, two forklifts, two AMRs) shuttling between the order's ends, obeying the same zone states | "Operations" button over the 3D view | Simulated. No collision avoidance between vehicles |
| Order and picking flow: orders arrive, a vehicle carries each from storage to packing, it is packed and dispatched; an outbound truck leaves every four orders and an inbound truck tops up the emptiest rack slots | Operations panel in the right column; trailer pulls away in the 3D view | Simulated |
| Floor workers walking fixed loops | 3D view | Scenery only: not observed and not part of routing |
| Live KPIs: deliveries, average delivery and waiting time, reroutes, blocked events, vehicles, stock fill | Strip at the top of the 3D view | Counted from the session; times are simulated seconds |
| Analytics: orders completed per minute, share of vehicles moving and orders waiting, charted over the last five minutes | Operations panel | Simulated, sampled every 2 s |
| Alerts: blocked or unknown zones, vehicles holding, long order queue, low stock | Operations panel | Zone alerts follow the real zone states; the rest is simulated |
| Traffic heat: lanes tinted lime by how much vehicle time they carried (red stays reserved for blocked zones) | "Traffic heat" button (when Operations is on) | Simulated |
| Inventory: stock per rack slot, low-stock highlighting, units taken by each order | Click a rack in the 3D view | Simulated stock, not a warehouse system feed |
| Stock heatmap: racks tinted red to lime by fill level | "Stock heatmap" button | Simulated stock |
| Evidence timeline: every zone change keeps the frame that caused it | Event timeline | Real in LIVE mode |
| Calibration with a held-out check | Camera panel: Calibrate, Verify | Real |
| Accelerator panel: device, stack versions, GPU memory and load, per-stage latency | Right column | Real, read from the device |
| Fixed route vs replanning comparison | Right column | Simulated, computed on request |
| Wait vs reroute comparison with your own assumed clearance time | Order and decision, when rerouted or paused | Estimate from your assumption |

## Known limits

- A vehicle that is already inside a zone when it becomes blocked or unknown holds where it is until the zone is seen clear. With the fleet on, that can leave vehicles waiting inside an aisle; that is the intended conservative behaviour, not a fault.
- Vehicles and workers can pass through each other. The app demonstrates traffic reacting to observed zones, not fleet coordination.
- Inventory is generated from a fixed seed per use case. The cross-dock floor has no racking, so it has no inventory.
